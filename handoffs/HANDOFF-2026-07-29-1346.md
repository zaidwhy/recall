cloudflared tunnel:

## Known limitation (accepted, not a bug)

Render's free plan has no persistent disk - `data/` (ChromaDB memories, thumbnails) resets on
redeploy and likely on spin-down/wake after ~15 min idle. Fine for demo/personal use as-is;
revisit with a paid Starter + disk add-on if it becomes annoying.
